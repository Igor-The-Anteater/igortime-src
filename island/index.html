Welcome to Igor’s island.

I live here at night, I grow tofu ants, and I love music.

My games are in my house: walk into the door. Games give tofu seeds; plant them in the field and eat the ants that grow. Composers can visit the old music school, and the deep pool leads to the abyss.

As a guest your field and scores stay on this device. With an account you can dress up your Igor and keep everything everywhere.
